Step 1: Digital Encoding & Error Correction (Informatics)

Binary file data (0s & 1s) is converted to quaternary nucleotide bases (A, C, G, T) using fountain codes or Reed-Solomon redundancy.

Biochemical & Computational Mechanism:

Algorithmic constraint filtering avoids GC bias (<40% or >60%) and prevents homopolymer runs (e.g. AAAAA) that disrupt chemical synthesis.

👋 Welcome! 60-Second Guide

This app shows how DNA can be used as a computer hard drive. No technical knowledge needed.

Tab 1 — Molecular Translator

Type any message and watch it convert, letter by letter, into DNA code (A, C, G, T). Use the pink slider to simulate real-world errors, then press "Send to Decoder" to read the DNA back out — just like a real DNA storage machine does.

Tab 2 — Density Calculator

Pick a data size (your phone, Netflix, the whole internet) and see how little physical DNA it would weigh. Fun facts update automatically — great for showing friends.

Tab 3 — Storage Pipeline

Tap each of the 5 stages to learn how data actually goes into and comes out of DNA — from writing, to storing for 1,000 years, to reading back perfectly.

Tab 4 — Live Poll

Cast your vote on the future of DNA storage! Your vote is saved on your device and added to the live results.

🧬 Why DNA? One gram of DNA can theoretically hold ~215 Petabytes — all of the world's data in a shoebox, readable for 1,000+ years.
